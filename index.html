Our Story

Curating Excellence
Since 2015

LUX LIMITED was founded on a singular conviction: that great art deserves a great stage. We work exclusively with painters whose vision is bold, whose technique is masterful, and whose works will endure through generations.

Every piece is strictly limited — never mass produced, never duplicated beyond its stated edition. Our collectors acquire not just beauty, but true scarcity and lasting cultural value.

We are proud to represent artists like Marilina Marchica, whose poetic investigation of landscape, memory, and time has captured collectors across four continents.
